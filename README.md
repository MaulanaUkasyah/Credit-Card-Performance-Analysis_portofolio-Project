## 📌 Business Objective
This project is designed to monitor the financial performance of credit card services, evaluate transaction volumes, and assess credit risk by tracking delinquent accounts across various customer demographics. The dashboard provides stakeholders with actionable insights into customer behavior and portfolio health.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Google Looker Studio:** Data Visualization, Interactive Dashboards, Layout Preparation.
* **Data Source:** Financial and banking dataset provided by the training organizer (includes account status, transaction volume, and customer demographics).

## 💡 Key Insights
Through this interactive dashboard, several critical financial metrics were synthesized:
1. **Financial Overview:** Tracked a total transaction volume of 326,544, amounting to a total transaction value of over $22.3 Million[cite: 10].
2. **Risk Management:** Identified and monitored 317 delinquent accounts out of a total client base of 5,054 customers[cite: 10].
3. **Customer Behavior:** The average credit utilization ratio across all customers stands at 26.8%[cite: 10].
4. **Demographic Segmentation:** Analyzed key metrics such as interest earned and delinquency rates across various customer professions (e.g., Businessman, White-collar, Blue-collar, Retirees) and education levels to identify high-value customer segments[cite: 10].

# 🇮🇩 Versi Bahasa Indonesia

## 📌 Tujuan Bisnis
Proyek ini dirancang untuk memantau performa finansial dari layanan kartu kredit, mengevaluasi volume transaksi, serta menilai risiko kredit dengan melacak akun yang menunggak (*delinquent accounts*) di berbagai demografi nasabah. *Dashboard* ini memberikan wawasan yang dapat ditindaklanjuti oleh pemangku kepentingan terkait perilaku nasabah dan kesehatan portofolio.

## 🛠️ Alat & Teknologi
* **Google Looker Studio:** Data Visualization, Interactive Dashboards, Layout Preparation.
* **Sumber Data:** Dataset perbankan dan keuangan dari penyelenggara pelatihan (Mencakup status akun, volume transaksi, dan demografi nasabah).

## 💡 Wawasan Utama (*Key Insights*)
Melalui *dashboard* interaktif ini, beberapa metrik finansial penting berhasil dirangkum:
1. **Ringkasan Finansial:** Melacak total volume transaksi sebanyak 326.544, dengan total nilai transaksi melampaui $22,3 Juta[cite: 10].
2. **Manajemen Risiko:** Mengidentifikasi dan memantau 317 akun yang menunggak (*delinquent accounts*) dari total keseluruhan 5.054 nasabah[cite: 10].
3. **Perilaku Nasabah:** Rata-rata rasio penggunaan kredit (*average utilization ratio*) dari seluruh nasabah berada pada angka 26,8%[cite: 10].
4. **Segmentasi Demografi:** Menganalisis metrik utama seperti bunga yang diperoleh (*interest earned*) dan tingkat tunggakan berdasarkan profesi nasabah (misal: *Businessman*, *White-collar*, *Blue-collar*, *Retirees*) serta tingkat pendidikan untuk mengidentifikasi segmen nasabah bernilai tinggi[cite: 10].
